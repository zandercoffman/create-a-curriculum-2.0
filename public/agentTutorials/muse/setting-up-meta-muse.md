# Setting Up Meta Muse

Meta Muse is your personal AI agent for turning curiosity into something useful. Pick any topic and Muse will dig up the information, organize what actually matters, and shape it into a learning plan built around you.

It works the way you'd talk to a knowledgeable friend — just describe what you want to learn or make, and Muse handles the heavy lifting: gathering sources, structuring lessons, drafting outlines, and helping you stay organized as you go.

Whether you're building a full curriculum, researching a new skill, or just trying to understand something deeply, Muse meets you where you are and helps you move forward.

Here's what that looks like in practice:

- **Build curriculums** — turn any topic into a structured learning path: outlines, lesson sequences, resources, and assessments, organized the way you actually teach.
- **Research products** — compare options side by side, gather specs and reviews, and get a clear breakdown before you buy or recommend anything.
- **Set and track goals** — break big ambitions into concrete steps, with Muse helping you monitor your progress and stay accountable.
- **Run your personal feed** — get briefings on the topics you care about, written for you on your schedule, so you stay current without the doomscrolling.
- **Plan your week** — turn a busy list of responsibilities into a practical plan, helping you figure out what needs to happen and when.
- **Organize information** — take scattered notes, links, ideas, and resources and turn them into something clear and easy to use.
- **Simplify complicated topics** — ask Muse to research something confusing and explain it in plain language, with the important details organized for you.
- **Create learning resources** — generate study guides, worksheets, reading lists, discussion questions, quizzes, and other materials around what you're learning.
- **Create detailed, customized PDFs** — turn your ideas and information into polished, structured PDFs tailored to your needs, including guides, workbooks, planners, reports, lesson materials, and more.
- **Prepare for important tasks** — break down an upcoming project, meeting, presentation, or deadline into manageable steps so you know where to start.
- **Keep track of research** — have Muse gather information from multiple sources and organize the findings so you don't have to keep dozens of tabs open.
- **Turn ideas into action plans** — start with a rough idea and work with Muse to turn it into a realistic sequence of next steps.
- **Draft content** — create emails, announcements, lesson materials, explanations, posts, documents, and other content from a simple starting idea.
- **Brainstorm solutions** — work through decisions, problems, and ideas with Muse to explore different possibilities before settling on a direction.
- **Stay on top of recurring responsibilities** — create reminders, routines, and repeatable systems so fewer things have to live in your head.
- **And more** — organize messy notes, plan projects, research topics, brainstorm ideas, and work through complicated tasks. If it involves thinking something through, Muse can help.

## Who Made Meta Muse?

Muse was created by **Meta**, the company behind Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp. Depending on your account setup, you may be able to use your existing Meta, Facebook, or Instagram account to access Muse.

## How to Access Meta Muse

Before getting started, you may need to connect your Facebook and Instagram accounts through Meta's Accounts Center.

**Official Meta resources:**

[About Accounts Center](https://www.facebook.com/help/943858526073065) · [About Meta Accounts](https://www.meta.com/help/meta-account/876751767182450/) · [Facebook Help Center](https://www.facebook.com/help/search/?q=where%20is%20my%20profile%20link) · [Manage Profiles](https://www.meta.com/help/meta-account/835746998306273/)

> ⚠️ **Access to Muse may take some time.** Depending on availability, you may be placed on a waitlist before you can access Muse. If you don't get immediate access, don't worry — your account may simply need to wait for access to become available.

Once you're ready, open the [**Meta Muse website**](https://muse.ai) at muse.ai.

Sign in using the account or login method available to you. After signing in, you should see the Meta Muse start screen.
